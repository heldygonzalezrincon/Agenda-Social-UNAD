# **App Name**: Futuro Académico

## Core Features:

- Dynamic Article Display: Present reportajes and crónicas with distinct visual layouts, including large images, titles, author and date metadata, leads, and 'Read More' call-to-action buttons, designed in a modern digital magazine style for reports and a narrative visual style for chronicles.
- Responsive Site Navigation: Implement a user-friendly and responsive top menu featuring links to 'Inicio', 'Reportajes', 'Crónicas', 'Free Press', and 'Sobre el medio', ensuring seamless navigation across devices.
- Engaging Hero Section: Showcase a prominent hero section with the core message, 'La educación ya cambió. ¿Las universidades también?', a concise subtitle, an 'Explorar publicaciones' button, and a thematic dark background image with an elegant overlay.
- 'Sobre el Medio' Section: Present information about the medium's objective, ethical focus, and target audience using a modern, card-based design.
- Social-Style 'Free Press' Feed: Display content in an aesthetically pleasing Instagram-like format, featuring posts, relevant hashtags, simulated like and comment counts, and share functionalities.
- AI Content Insight Tool: An AI-powered tool that provides article summaries, identifies key takeaways, and suggests relevant hashtags or social media copy to enhance content promotion and user engagement.
- Curated Visual Gallery: A dedicated section to visually display images of students interacting with technology, digital classrooms, artificial intelligence concepts, and modern university environments.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A vibrant, professional blue (#5E9FF5) to signify innovation and technological advancement, used for interactive elements and key highlights.
- Background color: Dark slate blue (#0F172A) providing a sophisticated and elegant base for the digital magazine, creating a strong contrast for brighter elements.
- Accent color: A crisp, bright cyan (#17CDED) to add a technological edge and dynamic visual interest to specific features and animations.
- Secondary dark color: A soft, dark gray (#111827) for subtle distinctions in backgrounds, such as card elements or alternate sections.
- Secondary accent color: A deep red (#7F1D1D) for select calls to attention, warnings, or special emphasis without distracting from the main palette.
- Main text colors: White and light gray (#E5E7EB) for excellent readability against the dark backgrounds, adhering to a minimalist and elegant aesthetic.
- Headline font: 'Poppins', a geometric sans-serif, for a modern, precise, and avant-garde look in titles and prominent text elements.
- Body font: 'Montserrat', a sans-serif, for clear and professional readability in longer texts and general content throughout the platform.
- Utilize minimalist, modern line icons that subtly convey technological and academic themes, ensuring they blend elegantly with the overall professional design.
- Employ a responsive, grid-based layout for article displays and content sections, mimicking a high-end digital magazine with generous white space and structured element arrangement to highlight content.
- Ensure distinct visual treatments for each content section (Hero, About Us cards, Magazine-style Reports, Narrative Chronicles, Instagram-like Free Press, Image Gallery, Footer) while maintaining overall thematic consistency.
- Incorporate subtle and elegant hover effects for interactive elements (buttons, navigation links) and smooth transitions for section reveals to enhance the cinematic and modern feel without being distracting.
